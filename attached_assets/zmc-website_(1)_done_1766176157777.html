About ZMC

Learn about our mission, vision, and mandate

Home / About

Our Mission

Enhancing democracy by facilitating access to information and a diverse, plural and ethical media.

Our Vision

Fostering access to information and a professional, national interest-driven media by 2030.

Our Objective

To facilitate the development of an accessible and professional media.

Purpose & General Mandate

The Zimbabwe Media Commission (ZMC) is one of the five Chapter 12 Independent Commissions whose purpose is the entrenchment of a democratic society driven by respect for the Constitution, rule of law, democracy and human rights among other objectives.

The Commission's major focus is on the promotion and protection of freedom of expression and the media; and the promotion of accountable governance through facilitating public access to information held by public entities for the purposes of transparency, accountability and protection of human rights.

Functions of the ZMC

Section 249 of the Constitution spells out the functions of the Commission:

  • To uphold, promote and develop freedom of the media
  • To promote and enforce good practices and ethics in the media
  • To monitor broadcasting in the public interest
  • To encourage the formulation of codes of conduct
  • To receive and consider complaints from the public
  • To ensure people have fair and wide access to information
  • To encourage use of all officially recognised languages
  • To encourage adoption of new technology in media
  • To promote fair competition and diversity in media
  • To conduct research into issues relating to freedom of press

About Accreditation

Accreditation of journalists and other people employed in the media is provided by Statutory Instrument 169C of 2002. The Statutory Instrument prescribes the manner and form in which an individual can lodge an application for accreditation with the Zimbabwe Media Commission. Statutory Instrument 65 of 2022 prescribes the fee payable for accreditation.

How to Apply

1

Download Form

Get the accreditation application form from our downloads section

2

Gather Documents

Prepare all required documents listed below

4

Submit Application

Submit your application via the portal or in person

Local Media Practitioners Requirements

  • Certified copy of National Identity card
  • One passport size photograph
  • Certified copy of professional and academic qualifications
  • Where certificates are not available, five samples of past work done
  • Stamped letter from employer or media house or professional association
  • Accreditation fee as prescribed

Foreign Media Practitioners Requirements

  • Copy of a valid passport
  • One passport size photograph
  • Temporary Employment Permit (TEP) / Clearance from Ministry of Information
  • Accreditation fee as prescribed

Accreditation Fee

As per SI 65 of 2022

About Registration

Registration of mass media services is provided by Statutory Instrument 169C of 2002. The Statutory Instrument prescribes the manner and form in which an individual can lodge an application for registration with the Zimbabwe Media Commission. Statutory Instrument 65 of 2022 prescribes the fee payable for registration.

How to Register

1

Download Form

Get the registration application form from our downloads section

2

Prepare Documents

Compile all required documents as listed below

3

Pay Fee

Pay the prescribed registration fee

4

Submit & Review

Submit application and await ZMC review

Local Media Organizations Requirements

  • Projected cash flow statement for three years
  • Projected balance sheet for three years
  • Editorial charter
  • Code of ethics
  • Code of conduct for employees
  • Market analysis
  • Certified IDs for directors
  • In-house style book
  • Dummy (copy of publication)
  • Mission statement
  • Certificate of incorporation
  • Memorandum of association

Foreign Media Representative Office Requirements

  • Permission to operate from Ministry of Information, Publicity and Broadcasting Services
  • Proof of registration in their country
  • Names and certified IDs of people running the representative office
  • Contact details (Physical address, email and telephones)

Registration Fee

As per SI 65 of 2022

About Complaints

The Commission receives complaints against media practitioners and media organizations that would have breached media laws and ethics. Individuals are encouraged to lodge a formal complaint in writing.

What Can You Complain About?

  • Breach of media ethics and professional standards
  • Defamation or invasion of privacy
  • Biased or inaccurate reporting
  • Unprofessional conduct by journalists
  • Violation of media laws

About Appeals (FOIA)

Under the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA), the Zimbabwe Media Commission is required to review decisions relating to access to information held by public entities. The Commission receives appeals from members of the public who are denied access to requested information as per the provisions of the law.

When Can You Appeal?

  • When a public entity refuses to provide requested information
  • When there is unreasonable delay in providing information
  • When you believe the fees charged are excessive
  • When information provided is incomplete
